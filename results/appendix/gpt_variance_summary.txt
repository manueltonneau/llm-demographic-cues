GPT-5.2 RUN-TO-RUN VARIANCE (App. B.3)
=======================================================
Design: 150 prompts x 8 cue conditions x 3 tasks, k=5 repetitions, temperature 1
(no seed; the OpenAI API exposes none). 18,000 calls, 0 failed. batch_6a4f555f...

AGGREGATE run-to-run stability (SD of the per-condition mean across the 5 runs):
  - Binary tasks (affirmative rate): SD <= 0.021 (medical <=0.013, legal <=0.021).
  - Salary (mean recommendation):    SD <= 0.72% of the mean (<= ~$680).
This is roughly an order of magnitude smaller than the cross-cue differences we
report (e.g. medical affirmative rate spans ~0.72-0.92 across cues; salary spans
~$82k-94k), so sampling stochasticity does not drive the cue effects.

Per-prompt (single-response) SD is naturally larger and is in
gpt_variance_sd_by_condition.csv.
